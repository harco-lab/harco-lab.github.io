## Research Presentation

### ■ Spatial and Semantic Reasoning for LLM-Driven Robot Navigation via MCP

**Authors:** Jungsoo Lee, Jaegyun Park, and Wansoo Kim  
**Affiliation:** Hanyang University, Republic of Korea

HARCO Lab presented research titled **"Spatial and Semantic Reasoning for LLM-Driven Robot Navigation via MCP."**

The study proposes a framework that integrates **Large Language Models (LLMs)** with existing **ROS-based robot navigation systems** using the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**.

The proposed framework enables LLMs to interpret spatial and semantic information, understand natural-language instructions, and determine appropriate navigation targets without modifying the existing ROS navigation architecture.

Experimental results in a simulated indoor environment demonstrated **over 97% map coverage** during autonomous mapping, along with successful spatial and semantic reasoning-based navigation.
