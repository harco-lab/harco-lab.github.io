On **November 5, 2026**, researchers from **Centria University of Applied Sciences, Finland**, visited **HARCO Lab at Hanyang University ERICA** to exchange research ideas and explore recent developments in robotics and intelligent systems.

During the visit, HARCO Lab introduced its ongoing research projects and demonstrated various robotic systems developed in the laboratory. The demonstrations highlighted the lab's research activities and technological developments across multiple areas of robotics.
