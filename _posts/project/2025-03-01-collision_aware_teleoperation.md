#### Goal of This Project

- Keep the robot safe during VR teleoperation by avoiding self-collisions and collisions with the environment.
- Reflect the demonstrator's whole upper-limb motion, including the elbow, in the robot's motion.
- Build and validate additional demonstration datasets for learning manipulation skills.

***

#### Project Contents

- Real-time self-collision and environment collision avoidance during VR teleoperation
- Retargeting of the demonstrator's hand pose and elbow position to the dual-arm hand robot
- Demonstration datasets for tasks such as can sorting and book shelving, validated by training skill-learning policies
