#### Goal of This Project

- Develop a VR teleoperation system that lets people control a dual-arm robot with dexterous hands easily and intuitively.
- Build a dataset of expert demonstrations for learning the robot's manipulation skills.

***

#### Project Contents

- VR hand teleoperation that retargets the user's hand, wrist and elbow motion to two Kinova Gen3 arms with Allegro Hands in real time
- Teleoperation of both the real robot and its simulation on ROS 2
- Real-robot demonstration datasets for household tasks such as pick-and-place, table wiping and lifting a pot with both hands
