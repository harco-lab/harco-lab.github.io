# Robot Learning Research

We conduct robot learning research for human-robot collaboration. Our work focuses on imitation learning and reinforcement learning for robot control, human intent recognition, and LLM- and VLM-based robot control. Through these efforts, we aim to enable robots to understand human needs and respond in real time, realizing more natural and effective human-robot collaboration.
